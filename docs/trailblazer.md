# Trail Blazer

Trail Blazer is PedalWorks' second bicycle model, assembled alongside the City Cruiser on the same Portland Assembly Floor. It shares two of its three parts with the City Cruiser: the Frame, bought from FrameForge Ltd, and the Wheelset, bought from RollRight Co.

The one part Trail Blazer does not share is the Suspension Fork. The Suspension Fork comes from a new supplier, ForkFactory Co, with a 12 day lead time and a unit cost of $150 — the most expensive single part PedalWorks buys. In this corpus, Trail Blazer's bill of materials is just those three parts: the Frame, the Wheelset, and the Suspension Fork — it is not built with a Drivetrain or a Brake Set of its own.

Building a Trail Blazer works the same way as building a City Cruiser: Assembly consumes one of each of the three parts from `PartsDB` and posts the labor cost of putting them together to the Ledger. Once assembled, a Trail Blazer moves on to Quality for the same QC inspection every City Cruiser goes through, then on to Shipping.
