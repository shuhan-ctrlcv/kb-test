# Bill of materials

This page breaks each purchased part down one level further: the material
it is made of, and the sub-parts it is built from. `docs/procurement.md`
covers the part as a single purchased unit with a supplier, a lead time,
and a unit cost; this page covers what is inside that unit.

| Part | Material | Sub-parts |
|---|---|---|
| Frame | Aluminium | Main Tube Set, Head Tube, Welded Joints |
| Wheelset | Alloy | Rims, Spokes, Hub, Tyres |
| Drivetrain | Steel | Chain, Cassette, Crankset, Derailleur |
| Brake Set | Steel & Rubber | Calipers, Brake Pads, Levers |
| Suspension Fork | Carbon Fibre | Stanchions, Lowers, Spring/Damper |

**Frame.** Made of Aluminium. It breaks down into three sub-parts: the Main
Tube Set (the main frame tubes), the Head Tube (where the fork steers
through), and the Welded Joints (where the tubes are joined to each other).

**Wheelset.** Made of Alloy. It breaks down into four sub-parts: Rims,
Spokes, Hub, and Tyres — the standard set of parts that together make up a
wheel.

**Drivetrain.** Made of Steel. It breaks down into four sub-parts: Chain,
Cassette, Crankset, and Derailleur — the parts that move power from the
rider's legs to the wheel.

**Brake Set.** Made of Steel & Rubber. It breaks down into three sub-parts:
Calipers, Brake Pads, and Levers.

**Suspension Fork.** Made of Carbon Fibre. It breaks down into three
sub-parts: Stanchions, Lowers, and Spring/Damper — the sliding tubes, the
lower legs they slide into, and the spring-and-damper unit that absorbs the
impact between them.

Only five parts have a bill of materials at all — Frame, Wheelset,
Drivetrain, Brake Set, and Suspension Fork — because those are the only five
parts PedalWorks buys from an outside supplier in the first place.
