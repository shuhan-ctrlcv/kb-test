# Quality

Quality is the PedalWorks stage that inspects every bicycle — City Cruiser or Trail Blazer — after it comes off the Portland Assembly Floor and before it is allowed to ship. This inspection is quality control, usually shortened to QC: a check that the assembled bicycle meets PedalWorks' build standard.

Running QC is not free, even when a bicycle passes. The inspection process consumes test material — for example destructive brake and weld checks — so every inspection posts a scrap cost to the Ledger regardless of whether the bicycle passes or fails. Quality is the third of the four stages that post a cost to the Ledger, alongside Procurement (purchase cost), Assembly (labor cost), and Shipping (freight cost).

Not every unit clears QC on the first attempt. The very first unit inspected in a run gets a first-article inspection — a stricter check manufacturers commonly run on the first unit off a new build — and it always fails. A unit that fails QC is not scrapped: it returns to Assembly for rework, and Assembly re-posts a labor cost to the Ledger for the extra work. Quality then inspects the reworked unit again before it can move on.

Once a bicycle clears Quality, it is ready to move on to Shipping, which sends it out from the South Distribution Center to the customer.
