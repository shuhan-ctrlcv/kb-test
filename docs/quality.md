# Quality

Quality is the PedalWorks stage that inspects every City Cruiser after it comes off the Portland Assembly Floor and before it is allowed to ship. This inspection is quality control, usually shortened to QC: a check that the assembled bicycle meets PedalWorks' build standard.

Running QC is not free, even when a bicycle passes. The inspection process consumes test material — for example destructive brake and weld checks — so every inspection posts a scrap cost to the Ledger regardless of whether the bicycle passes or fails. Quality is the third of the four stages that post a cost to the Ledger, alongside Procurement (purchase cost), Assembly (labor cost), and Shipping (freight cost).

Once a City Cruiser clears Quality, it is ready to move on to Shipping, which sends it out from the South Distribution Center to the customer.
